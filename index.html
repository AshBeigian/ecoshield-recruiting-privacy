Privacy Policy

Ecoshield Summer Recruiting, run by Jaxson Hofmann (Instagram: @jaxsonhof).
Last updated: November 10, 2026.

This policy explains what happens to the information you share when you message us from one of our ads on Instagram or Facebook. The short version: your information is used by Jaxson and his recruiting team to talk to you about the summer sales opportunity, and for nothing else.

What we collect

  • Your Instagram or Facebook name and the messages you send us.
  • The answers you choose in our automated chat, such as what you are doing right now, your availability next summer, your work experience, and whether you played sports.
  • Your phone number, only if you choose to give it to us.

How we use it

  • To understand whether the opportunity is a fit for you, and to reply to you.
  • To contact you about the opportunity, including setting up a call if you ask for one.
  • To improve how we recruit. We reserve the right to review the answers we receive, together with the results of our ads, to refine our outreach, our questions and our advertising so we reach the right people. This is done inside our recruiting team only.

What we will never do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone.
  • We do not use it for any commercial purpose other than recruiting you for this opportunity.
  • We do not use it to target you with ads for other products or services, and we do not hand it to other companies to do so.

Phone number and contacting you

If you give us your phone number, you agree that Jaxson or his recruiting team may contact you by call or text message about this opportunity. We will only reach out for that reason and we will not spam you. Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. You can stop at any time by replying STOP to a text or by telling us in a direct message, and we will not contact you again.

Who can see it

Only Jaxson and his recruiting team. Meta Platforms (Instagram and Facebook) also processes the messages under its own privacy policy, because the conversation happens on their apps.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it to recruit for the current and next season, then we delete it.

Your choices

You can ask at any time to see the information you sent us, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Send a direct message to @jaxsonhof on Instagram and we will take care of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
